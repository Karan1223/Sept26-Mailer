HYDROGEN-POWERED JCB HYDROMAX SETS 406.320 MPH WORLD LAND SPEED RECORD-JCB.

JCB has set a new world land speed record with its hydrogen-powered JCB Hydromax, reaching an average speed of 406.320 mph (653.5 km/h) at the Bonneville Salt Flats. Powered by two production-based JCB hydrogen engines delivering a combined 1,600 bhp, Hydromax comfortably surpassed the previous hydrogen internal-combustion record of 185.5 mph. Driven by Wing Commander Andy Green OBE, who also drove JCB Dieselmax to its 350.092 mph diesel record in 2006, the achievement demonstrates the potential of hydrogen combustion technology for high-performance, zero-CO₂ tailpipe-emission applications.
